⚠ Heatwave System Overview

Integrating real-time meteorological tracking with high-level advisory distribution.

About the System

Objective

The system aims to provide early, reliable, and region-specific warnings of heatwave conditions, enabling timely response by meteorologists, disaster-management officers, and the general public.

Data Sources

Forecasts are generated using data from Automatic Weather Stations (AWS), the IMD historical database, and real-time satellite feeds, combined through an AI-based prediction model for higher accuracy.

Stakeholders

Key stakeholders include meteorologists who validate forecasts, disaster-management officers who coordinate ground response, and citizens who receive advisories to stay safe during severe heat events.

"Timely and accurate heatwave intelligence saves lives. This system bridges the gap between raw weather data and actionable public advisories."
— Chief Meteorologist, Disaster Management Cell
